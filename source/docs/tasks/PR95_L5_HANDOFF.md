## 2. Root causes and narrow fixes

1. **KPI completion mismatch:** `getKpis` previously counted only raw `Hoàn thành`. Council/NQ/BC reports counted normalized canonical `Đã trình HĐND thành phố` plus `Hoàn thành`, `Đã trình HĐND` and `Đã trình HĐND TP`. Result: old/new accepted aliases could disagree between the KPI and the report. Fix: use the **existing** `normalizeReportStatus` and Council configuration for KPI Council status, not an invented status. Blank Council status remains `Sở chưa trình`; unknown labels remain unknown.
2. **False replacement changes from row indices:** `previewDatasetReplacement` previously included `stt` in comparison despite matching by `displayCode`. Reordering/re-numbering the new Excel could mark unchanged business data as changed. Fix: exclude row-order `stt` and generation-local `recordId/revision` from content equality; keep editable business fields (including `isDropped/documentKind`) and `archivedAt`. Trim code only for key/duplicate checks; no arbitrary case folding or data mutation. A reused code with different content is **changed**, not added or removed.

No business category or official workflow stage was added. Default scopes for `Không phải trình` and empty construction progress remain unchanged.
